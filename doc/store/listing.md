# Store listing

Texts for Google Play, App Store and RuStore. CI never uploads them (see `doc/release.md`):
paste them into each console. Lengths are checked against the strictest store limit
(App Store name and subtitle 30, Play short description 80, keywords 100, description 4000).

Replace the demo-account placeholder in the review notes with a real account that has a
populated demo organisation before submitting: both Apple and Google reject apps they
cannot sign in to.

### Full description — 870/4000

```text
ACITS is free record-keeping software for animal shelters and rescue organisations.

Every animal in the shelter in one registry:
• animal profile: photos, species and breed, markings, intake and outcome history;
• prescriptions: vaccinations, parasite treatments, courses of treatment with doses and schedules;
• Today — what has to be done across all animals today;
• a monthly calendar of scheduled treatments;
• the shelter's medication catalogue with stock levels;
• curators and prospective adopters, comments and documents for each animal;
• the animal profile as a PDF, to send to a vet or to future owners.

Works on phones, tablets and in the browser: on a wide screen the list and the profile sit side by side.

Staff permissions are set per organisation: administrator, worker, guest.

An organisation account at acits.ru is required. The app is open source.
```

## Screenshots

Required per store: phone (6.7" and 6.5" for App Store; at least two for Google Play),
iPad 13" (the app supports iPad), Play 7" and 10" tablets. Suggested set, in both languages:

1. Today — the day's treatments.
2. Animals — the list (phone) / list and card side by side (tablet).
3. Animal card with photos.
4. Prescription form.
5. Calendar month.
6. Drugs catalogue with stock.

Take them from a demo organisation with realistic but fictional data: no real names, phones
or addresses of people.
